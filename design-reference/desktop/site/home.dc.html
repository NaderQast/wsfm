How every job runs

The same five steps on every job, so what you’re quoted is what gets delivered and documented.

Start with a quote
  1. 1

    Audit

    Tell us about the property. We review the scope and flag any safety or presentation issues to address.

  2. 2

    Quote and plan

    We confirm the quote and, for ongoing work, set a maintenance plan and schedule.

  3. 3

    Clean and maintain

    The agreed cleaning or maintenance work is carried out to a consistent standard.

  4. 4

    QA inspection

    A quality check confirms the work meets the agreed standard.

  5. 5

    Sign-off and records

    The job is signed off and you receive the photos and documentation.
